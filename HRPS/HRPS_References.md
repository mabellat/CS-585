# HRPS — References

Numbered sources used in the study docs. The paper is **`[P]`**. The CSCI 585 lecture deck is **`[L]`** (`HRPS.pdf`).

| ID | Source |
|----|--------|
| **P** | Shahram Ghandeharizadeh and David J. DeWitt. *Hybrid-Range Partitioning Strategy: A New Declustering Strategy for Multiprocessor Database Machines.* **VLDB 1990**, Brisbane. Pages 481–492 in the proceedings. Local copy: [`Hybrid-Range.PDF`](Hybrid-Range.PDF). |
| **L** | CSCI 585 lecture: *Hybrid-Range Partitioning Strategy*, Shahram Ghandeharizadeh and David DeWitt. Local copy: [`HRPS.pdf`](HRPS.pdf) (97 slides). |
| **R1** | Michael Stonebraker. *The Case for Shared Nothing.* Database Engineering 9(1), March 1986. Cited as [STON86]. |
| **R2** | David J. DeWitt et al. *The Gamma Database Machine Project.* IEEE TKDE, March 1990. Cited as [DEWI90]. Gamma is the research vehicle. |
| **R3** | George Copeland, William Alexander, Ellen Boughter, and Tom Keller. *Data Placement in Bubba.* SIGMOD 1988. Cited as [COPE88]. Heat, temperature, and placement heuristics. |
| **R4** | Dina Bitton, David J. DeWitt, and Carolyn Turbyfill. *Benchmarking Database Systems — A Systematic Approach.* VLDB 1983. Wisconsin Benchmark relations used in the workload. |
| **R5** | Shahram Ghandeharizadeh, David J. DeWitt, and Waheed Qureshi. *MAGIC: A Multiattribute Declustering Mechanism for Multiprocessor Database Machines.* SIGMOD 1992. DOI [10.1145/141484.130293](https://doi.org/10.1145/141484.130293). Lecture follow-on, not the HRPS paper. |
| **R6** | Shahram Ghandeharizadeh and David J. DeWitt. *Performance Analysis of Alternative Declustering Strategies.* ICDE 1990. Cited as [GHAN90]. Range is best for full scans. |

**Pedagogical examples** on the slides (student grades 0–100, Emp salary/age pictures, ATM vs coffee-shop analogies for closed vs open models) are lecture illustrations **`[L]`**, not separate publications.
